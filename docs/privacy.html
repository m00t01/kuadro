Privacy policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

🇪🇸 Kuadro is also available in Spanish.

Kuadro is a desktop application that lets you view and edit your own Google Sheets as tables, boards, galleries, calendars and pages. This policy explains what data Kuadro accesses and what it does with it.

No Kuadro servers

Kuadro has no backend. The app runs on your computer and talks directly to Google's APIs. The developer of Kuadro never receives, stores or sees your data.

Google data Kuadro accesses

  • Google Sheets (spreadsheets scope): to read and write the Sheets you connect to Kuadro, including the hidden tabs Kuadro creates in them to store settings, page content, comments, history and presence.
  • Google Drive (drive.file scope): only to upload images you choose (thumbnails, covers, pictures in pages) to a folder in your Drive. Kuadro can only access files it created.
  • Basic profile (openid, email, profile): to show your name and to match you with the roles and permissions defined in each project.

How the data is used

Data is used only to provide Kuadro's features to you and to the people you share your Sheets with. It isn't sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising or used to train AI models. Kuadro's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

What is stored on your computer

Your sign-in token (encrypted with your operating system's secure storage when available), the list of projects you added and your personal preferences (language, theme, background, keyboard shortcut). Images you pick as a background are stored only on your computer.

Optional YouTube data

If a row contains a YouTube link, Kuadro fetches that video's public title and thumbnail from YouTube. If you add your own YouTube API key in the settings, it also fetches the public view count.

Removing your data

Sign out in Kuadro and uninstall the app to remove local data. To revoke Kuadro's access to your Google account, visit myaccount.google.com/permissions. Data stored in your Sheets and Drive stays under your control and can be deleted there.
